Data analytics projects

Microsoft Excel

Beyond Cars Limited

Car sales dashboard · 2020 data

Analyzed 1,491 car sales across six branches in South-West Nigeria. I cleaned the raw sales records, modeled them into a fact table with date, car, branch, age and gender dimensions, and built an interactive dashboard with pivot tables, slicers, month-on-month growth and car images that change with the selection.

  • Monthly sales grew from 70 in January to a peak of 200 in November.
  • Lagos was the top branch with 24% of sales, ahead of Osun and Ogun.
  • Buyers aged 26 to 35 made 63% of all purchases.
  • Toyota Corolla (201), Ford Fiesta (200) and Honda Civic (198) were the best sellers.
Excel · Data cleaning · Data modeling · Pivot tables · Dashboard

44Underwoods Property

Sales performance analysis · Aug 2023

Tracked 225 sales leads for a property company to find what turns a lead into a paying client. I broke conversion down by sales executive, client gender, week, location, product type and site inspection, and summarized it on a dashboard with a speedometer gauge for overall conversion.

  • 44% of leads became paying clients overall.
  • Leads who inspected the property converted at 55%, against 11% for those who didn't.
  • Residential property converted at 65%, commercial at 35%, and industrial had no sales.
  • Lagos leads converted at 51%, compared with 35% in Port Harcourt.
Excel · Pivot tables · Conversion analysis · Gauge chart · Dashboard
